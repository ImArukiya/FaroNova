Quick answers

FAQ.

Short answers for viewers, collaborators, and anyone landing here from a bio link.

What does FaroNova stream?

Gaming, reaction sessions, viewer playalongs, and anime watchalongs. Check Twitch for the active category.

Where is the latest content?

Twitch is the main platform — past VODs and clips are available right on the channel page after each stream ends.

How do I get notified when a stream starts?

Hit Follow on Twitch and enable notifications in the app. You can also follow on X for quick stream-start posts.

How do I join the community?

Use the Discord link above for the community server, stream updates, and shared clips.

How do I reach out for collaborations?

Use the email link in the community section or send a direct message on X.

Are VODs saved after streams?

Yes — past broadcasts are saved on the Twitch channel. Highlights and clips are also available from standout moments.
