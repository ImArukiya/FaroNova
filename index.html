Quick answers

FAQ.

Short answers for viewers, collaborators, and anyone landing here from a bio link.

What games does FaroNova play?

Mainly gacha games — Neverness to Everness, Wuthering Waves, Genshin Impact, and Zenless Zone Zero are regulars. Also plays Roblox and plenty of other games. Variety is the vibe.

Where is FaroNova from?

Malaysia. Streams are in English and the community welcomes everyone regardless of where you're from.

What is StreamerZone.gg?

FaroNova is an official streamer for StreamerZone.gg — a network and community built to help creators grow. Think of it as a home for up-and-coming streamers.

What's the stream vibe like?

Chill and welcoming. The goal is to create a safe space where you can hang out, talk about your day, and have fun. No pressure, no gatekeeping — just good company.

Where is the latest content?

TikTok gets the short-form clips and highlights. Twitch is where the live streams happen. YouTube has VODs and longer content. Follow all three to catch everything.

How do I reach out for collaborations?

Use the email link in the community section for business inquiries, campaigns, and partnerships. For casual collabs, slide into the DMs on X or Discord.
